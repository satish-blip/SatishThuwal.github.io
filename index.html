Building

An actuary-first pricing tool.

A personal project, in progress and independent of my employer. Much pricing software is designed around underwriting workflow or IT constraints. I'm building one around how pricing actuaries really work: judgement-heavy, audit-bound and short on time.

  1. 1Data & exposure
  2. 2Claims development
  3. 3Trend & inflation
  4. 4Stochastic & EVT simulation
  5. 5Layer & structure pricing

Judgement stays visible

Every method choice is shown side by side, and every override needs a written reason, so the actuary's judgement becomes part of the record rather than lost in a spreadsheet.

Every number traces back

Each calculation is reconciled against transparent Excel workings and a step-by-step audit trail, so a reviewer can follow any figure to its source.

Built for the tail

Simulation and extreme value methods are first-class rather than bolted on, because high-excess layers are where simple methods break.

Details are kept general here. Happy to talk through the approach.

Pricing leadership

Scaled Intact's US pricing team from inception. I sign off pricing for large accounts and high-excess layers, and I drive rate adequacy, mix-change and pricing-audit work across the portfolio.

Team of 10 · templates used across US & UK

02

Reinsurance & CAT

At Munich Re I priced property treaties for India and South-East Asia and underwrote large facultative risks, and developed and priced retail cyber, mobile and extended warranty products for digital partners. At Intact I founded the CAT Modelling Think Tank and oversee how CAT output feeds pricing decisions.

Treaty & facultative · CAT pricing

03

EVT & simulation

I lead the rollout of extreme value theory and simulation-based pricing for high-severity layers. The aim is pricing for those layers that is consistent and technically rigorous.

Stochastic modelling · EVT

I turn manual actuarial work into repeatable tools. At Munich Re I delivered 5+ automation and dashboarding projects in Excel and Power BI, and I now work in Python, SQL and VBA too, alongside machine learning from my IIT Bombay studies.

Python · SQL · Power BI · ML
